Cross-trades

Jobs that need more than one of HVAC, plumbing, electrical, or sewer on the same ticket. One lead, one estimate, split scopes — still catalog-only in v0.

catalog ops planned spans: hvac · plumbing · electrical · sewer

Overview

Field shops lose money when a “simple swap” quietly needs a second license. Cross-trades is the place to name that up front. The runtime will eventually keep a single job id with child scopes so dispatch does not send one van for three trades.

Use a single-trade module when the other work is only a courtesy note. Use this module when another trade can stop the job.

Common multi-trade jobs

Bath / kitchen remodel

Plumbing and electrical rough-in, sometimes HVAC exhaust. Sequence the opens.

HVAC change-out + power

New disconnect, breaker, or whip. HVAC does not invent the electrical scope.

Water heater swap

Plumbing plus vent, gas, or a dedicated circuit. List each exclusion.

Flood / backup restoration

Sewer or supply leak, then electrical drying and HVAC dehumidification.

New-construction rough-in

One walk, four possible trades. Hold one schedule owner.

Pad, disconnect, condensate

The outdoor unit is never “just HVAC” when the circuit or drain is wrong.
