Module · sewer

Sewer

Building drain, sewer lateral, and main-line work beyond an ordinary fixture clog. Camera footage and distance marks are first-class notes, even while the ops remain planned.

catalog ops planned author: Aziel Eliab

Overview

Sewer tickets are access, location, and evidence. A clear that does not record what the camera saw becomes a repeat call. Fixture-only stoppages stay on plumbing. When a backup floods a finished space, restoration and electrical drying belong on cross-trades.

Common job types

Main-line backup

Which fixtures are dead, where it first showed, and whether the city main is involved.

Camera inspection

Distance, grade clues, and a still or clip at every defect. No invented footage.

Cable or jet

Machine choice follows pipe size and debris. Record what came back.

Cleanout access

Locate, cut-in, or advise when there is no honest access.

Roots

Clear vs. treat vs. replace. Say which one the customer actually bought.

Spot repair estimate

Depth, surface, and whether excavation is the next trade.

Safety notes

  • Sewage exposure: PPE, splash, and decontamination on the van.

Checklist stub

stub
