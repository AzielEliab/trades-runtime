Module · electrical

Electrical

Service, branch-circuit, and equipment-power work for homes and light commercial sites. The module stays honest: v0 does not sell a live panel-design API.

catalog ops planned author: Aziel Eliab

Overview

Electrical tickets range from a dead outlet to a service conversation. The runtime will keep panel identity, circuit labels, and “was it de-energized and proven” on the work order. That is the difference between a callback and a documented repair.

HVAC disconnects, water-heater circuits, and remodel rough-ins belong on cross-trades when another trade owns part of the equipment.

Common job types

Device and lighting

Outlets, switches, fixtures. Confirm the circuit, not just the device in the hand.

Panel troubleshooting

Trips, heat, labeling, and whether the load or the breaker is the story.

Dedicated circuit

Path, fill, and termination. Estimate includes fishing and finish.

EV / generator interconnect

Interlock or transfer equipment, labeling, and utility/permit notes.

Equipment power

Disconnects and whip work that other trades request. Keep their ticket linked.

Service upgrade

Scoped only. v0 records the job type; it does not calculate a service.

Checklist stub

stub

  • Identify panel, circuit, and load before removing a cover.
  • Photograph existing labeling and damage.
  • Test the repaired circuit and restore or correct labels.
  • If HVAC or plumbing equipment is the load, link the other module.
