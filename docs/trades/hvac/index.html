How this domain sits on the OS

No-cool / no-heat volume is a weather-and-demand problem (v0.1 §25) before it is a route problem. Maintenance capacity is demand-first: sales opportunity weights Selling Techs on older systems, but a qualified available technician is dispatched rather than holding the customer for an ideal closer (§26). Electrical disconnects and condensate still belong on cross-trades when another license can stop the job.

Common call types

No-cool / no-heat

Diagnostic first. Indoor/outdoor temps and demand before parts talk.

Maintenance

Age-distributed through the maintenance/service ladder, not assigned blind.

Change-out

Often a multi-trade ticket: pad, disconnect, circuit, flue.

Leak / IAQ / controls

Call-Fit must include inventory, refrigerant credentials, and client-tech fit.
