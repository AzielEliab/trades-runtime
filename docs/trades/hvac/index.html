HVAC

Heating, ventilation, and air conditioning field work: diagnostics, maintenance, change-outs, and indoor-air jobs. This module is catalog-only in v0.

catalog ops planned author: Aziel Eliab

Overview

HVAC tickets usually start as comfort calls (no-cool, no-heat, noise, odor) or planned work (tune-up, replacement). The runtime will keep equipment identity, refrigerant, and authorization on the same work order so a callback is not a new guess.

Electrical disconnects, condensate, and gas/vent work often spill into cross-trades. Do not hide that on an HVAC-only ticket.

Common job types

No-cool / no-heat

Diagnostic first. Record indoor/outdoor temps, thermostat demand, and what the unit is doing before parts talk.

Maintenance tune-up

Filter, coils, drains, electrical draw, and a written exception list — not a silent “all good.”

Change-out / install

Load match, line-set, pad, flue, and startup readings. Permit notes stay on the estimate.

Leak search

Recovery, leak method, and whether the repair is worth the charge. No fake “top-off only” closeout.

Controls / thermostat

Wiring, communicating vs. conventional, and whether the complaint is the box or the plant.

Airflow / IAQ

Static pressure, returns, and filtration. Call electrical or carpentry when the duct is the job.

Safety notes

placeholder

  • Lock out the disconnect before opening an air handler or condenser.
  • Recover refrigerant; do not vent. Credentials belong on the work order later.
  • Treat gas heat as a combustion job: flue, CO, and heat-exchanger notes.
  • This list is a stub. It is not a substitute for shop policy or code.

Checklist stub

stub

  • Confirm call type, access, and equipment location.
  • Photograph nameplate and existing damage.
  • Write temps, static, and amperage before recommending parts.
  • Get authorization before recovery, repair, or change-out.
