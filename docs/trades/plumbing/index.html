Module · plumbing

Plumbing

Supply, drain, fixture, and water-heater work inside the building envelope. Main-line and lateral backups live on the sewer module so a faucet leak does not share a ticket shape with a collapsed lateral.

catalog ops planned author: Aziel Eliab

Overview

Plumbing jobs are water, waste, and fixtures: what is leaking, what is stopped, and whether the water heater or a hidden line is the real plant. The runtime will keep shutoff locations and finish-protection notes on the work order because reopen cost is usually the customer’s wall, not the valve.

Gas water heaters, recirculation pumps, and flooded rooms often need electrical or HVAC. Use cross-trades when more than one license is on site.

Common job types

Leak locate and repair

Isolate, open only what you must, and photograph wet structure before and after.

Fixture stoppage

Trap, branch, or vent — not automatically a main-line sewer call.

Water heater

Service, flush, or replace. T&P, drain, combustion or circuit, and expansion notes.

Fixture install

Valve condition, supply lines, and whether the drain is the hidden job.

Repipe / re-route

Estimate by zone. Call out access, finish work, and downtime.

Backflow / isolation

Device type, test tags, and who owns the annual test later.
