Plumbing

Supply, drain, fixture, and water-heater work inside the building envelope. Main-line and lateral backups live on sewer so a faucet leak does not share a ticket shape with a collapsed lateral.

How this domain sits on the OS

Precipitation, freeze-thaw, and (only if they earn weight) experimental lunar/Earth-tide features may inform plumbing demand — they must not be assumed to cause residential leaks (§25.3). Water-heater swaps with vent, gas, or circuit work are cross-trades. Plumbing → HVAC turnovers are a first-class analytics slice (v0.7).
