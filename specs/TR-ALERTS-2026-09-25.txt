 What changed
 0.4.2 adds local threshold rules on the operator desk. The rules read scores the desk already shows.
 They do not invent an accuracy percent. They do not phone home. They do not write to ServiceTitan,
 ProBooks, or a trades app.

 Rules
    capacity             Open slots at or below a local count, only when the desk has a lane.
                         A BYO desk with blank open slots waits. No utilization percent is filled in.
    late jobs            Unfinished jobs before the mission day, plus same-day unfinished jobs
                         after a configured fraction of the day. A count, not an on-time percent.
    trust-band           Evidence-trust score at or below a floor, or a drop remembered in local state.
                         n/a does not count as a company drop. Trust is not truth.
    booking block        recommendBlock is one of the configured blocks.
    verification stall   Verification score stays in the configured statuses for N minutes
                         measured from the oldest observation clock. No clock, no invented stall.
                         Wrapper admission is not verification.

 Desk
 Active rules render in the in-desk banner and in the alerts panel.
 History stays in a local state file. Acknowledge is a local POST on 127.0.0.1.
 Acknowledged rules leave the banner and stay in history.
 Labels stay synthetic demo, BYO-admitted, or BYO-admitted synthetic drill.

 Config
 Copy data/runtime/alerts.json.example to data/runtime/<instanceId>/alerts.json (gitignored).
 data/inbound/local.json may set alertsPath, or an alerts object, on this machine.
 Missing file uses the same defaults as the example. Hooks default off.

 Hooks
    file                 Append one JSON line per newly raised rule to a local path.
    webhook              POST that same line to loopback only (127.0.0.1, localhost, ::1).
 Any other host is refused. No phone-home. No tenant body. live_backends false. writes false.
