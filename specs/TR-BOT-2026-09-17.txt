 2. What the product is
 A local-first, shadow-first company OS for multi-trade field service. ServiceTitan remains the system of record. Trades-Runtime
 recommends, freezes evidence, compares later, and never silently takes the wheel. Standing rule in README: every PDF Aziel
 sends is a spec to implement as coded software. Implementer specs live in specs/. Pages UI lives in docs/. Product lives in src/.
 Not: a dispatch optimizer, a revenue-as-skill leaderboard, a fixed morning router, an autonomous black box, photo-as-misconduct
 proof, fuel-first routing, an automatic 30-day callback penalty, a listing scraper, an AVM, or a public mesh node.

     Drift                       Route drift rebases from actual state. Drift is evidence, not automatic failure.

     Bookings                    Existing commitments have precedence. Silent displace is forbidden.
