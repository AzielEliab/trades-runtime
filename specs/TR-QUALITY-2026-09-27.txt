 What this is
 Three local Softwares on the operator desk.

 1. Inbound quality report
    ServiceTitan and ProBooks fragments already admitted as peer classes.
    The desk reads files on this machine. It does not pull a tenant.
    Flags: completeness, schema fit, stale or missing fields, conflicting keys,
    thin evidence, and refused writes.
    The checklist score is not an accuracy percent.
    Human text and machine JSON:
      data/runtime/<instanceId>/inbound-quality.txt
      data/runtime/<instanceId>/inbound-quality.json
      data/runtime/<instanceId>/inbound-quality.jsonl
    Loopback: /api/inbound-quality and /api/inbound-quality.txt
    Charts: volume by sourceKind, score distribution, top defect classes.
    An empty folder shows a labeled synthetic demo. That demo is not a company export.

 2. Alert to action stubs
    When a local alert rule is firing, the desk shows proposed actions.
    Each stub has a label, a rationale, the required human authority,
    and refused: write-back.
    The Human Authority Rule is on the panel.
    Stubs persist at data/runtime/<instanceId>/alert-actions.json
    and alert-actions.jsonl.
    Loopback: /api/alert-actions
    Nothing calls ServiceTitan or ProBooks.

 3. Option C start gate
    A checklist of what must be true before a real pilot may start.
    Every gate stays blocked-until.
    pilot_started stays false. pilot may start stays false.
    This panel does not start a pilot.
    Option D is out of scope. No cutover automation.

 4. Monitoring view
    One local desk panel. Not five new menus.
    Pins for techs and trucks come from an optional positions file,
    from coordinates on a local miles file, or from a labeled synthetic demo.
    Mile totals still ignore coordinates. The address map stays undrawn.
    Drive cards, tech score cards, a call board, and KPI charts
    recompute when those local files change.
    Not a live GPS feed. Not a telematics vendor. live_backends stays false.
    Human Authority Rule stays on the panel. Monitoring only. No write-back.
    Loopback: /api/monitoring
    Example: data/runtime/positions.json.example
