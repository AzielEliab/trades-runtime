1. Time tracking
   Local cards for the current call: clocked, elapsed, estimated remaining,
   idle and travel segments. Optional file at
   data/runtime/<instanceId>/time-cards.json or data/inbound/time-cards.json.
   Copy data/runtime/time-cards.json.example.
   The desk recomputes from that file and the local clock.
   It writes data/runtime/<instanceId>/time-tracking.json and time-tracking.jsonl.
   Empty-folder desk shows a labeled synthetic demo.
   A refused file is not replaced by that demo.
   Not a live GPS feed. Not a telematics vendor.
   GET /api/time-tracking

2. Service coverage
   Zip codes, counties, cities, and roads or highways.
   Optional GeoJSON-ish file at data/runtime/<instanceId>/coverage.json
   or data/inbound/coverage.json.
   Copy data/runtime/coverage.json.example.
   Human on/off switches persist in coverage-layers.json on this machine.
   Breakdowns count places, jobs, and techs from the local file.
   Empty-folder desk shows a labeled synthetic demo.
   SVG on the Monitoring panel. The address map stays undrawn.
   Not a live map-tile vendor. Not a live GPS feed.
   GET /api/coverage
   POST /api/coverage/layers is a local switch only. vendorWrite false.

3. Right tech
   Suggestions for an open or scheduled job from geolocation, estimated time
   remaining on the current call, and skill fit.
   Friction and heavy local drive minutes are flags, not a dispatch.
   Geography is not the only factor.
   Human Authority Rule: suggestions only. No auto-dispatch. No write-back.
   GET /api/right-tech and GET /api/tech-fit. Optional ?job=

The three sit on the Monitoring panel. They are not new desk menus.
